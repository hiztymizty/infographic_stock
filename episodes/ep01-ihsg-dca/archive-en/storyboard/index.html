Picked · honest endingTone

The story lands on 1.7% a year, because that is what the price data says. Dividends are excluded and flagged on screen rather than estimated.

Method

Data: Yahoo Finance ^JKSE monthly bars, Jan 2010 to 9 Oct 2026. Each month buys Rp 5,000,000 of the index at that month's opening level; the portfolio is valued at each month's close and at the latest print (6,043) for today. Price index only: dividends, fees and taxes are not included, and you can't buy the index directly (an IHSG index fund is the real-world equivalent). The per-year figure is a money-weighted (XIRR) return.
